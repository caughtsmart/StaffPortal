## 1. Introduction

This policy applies to all employees who use personal devices (e.g. laptops, smartphones, tablets, or removable media) to access Loaded Dice systems, applications, or data.

The purpose of this policy is to protect the security and integrity of Loaded Dice information and technology infrastructure while allowing employees the flexibility to use personal devices for work.

This policy must be read in conjunction with the Communications, Email, and Internet Policy and the Data Protection Policy.

Employees may only connect personal devices to Loaded Dice systems with prior written approval from the Managing Director (or delegate) and by agreeing to comply with this policy.

## 2. Acceptable use

### 2.1 Permitted uses

Employees may use personal devices for:

- Business activities that support Loaded Dice operations.
- Reasonable and limited personal use (e.g. brief personal calls, reading, light recreation).

### 2.2 Prohibited uses

Devices must not be used to:

- Store or transmit unlawful, offensive, or discriminatory material.
- Store or transmit proprietary information belonging to another organisation.
- Harass, bully, or defame others.
- Conduct personal business activities.

Employees may access Loaded Dice resources (e.g. email, calendars, contacts, documents, and approved social media accounts) only through secure company-authorised applications.

## 3. Data protection and GDPR compliance

Loaded Dice is the data controller for all work-related personal data stored on employee devices. The Data Protection Officer (DPO) is responsible for overseeing compliance with this policy.

Employees must comply with GDPR principles when handling personal data, which means:

- Using data only for legitimate business purposes.
- Ensuring data is accurate, relevant, and up-to-date.
- Keeping data secure and deleting it when no longer needed.
- Reporting loss, unauthorised access, or suspected breaches immediately to the DPO.

Employees must not create, process, or store special category data (e.g. health, race, religion, sexual orientation) on personal devices. Any such data received must be deleted immediately and reported to the DPO.

## 4. Employees' obligations

### 4.1 Security

Employees must:

- Protect devices with a strong password, PIN, or biometric lock.
- Ensure devices lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity.
- Configure devices to lock after several failed password attempts.
- Use antivirus/anti-malware software and keep operating systems updated.
- Transfer company data only via encrypted channels (e.g. VPN).
- Avoid downloading unverified or insecure applications.
- Avoid connecting to unsecured Wi-Fi networks.
- Report any device loss, theft, or suspected breach immediately to the DPO.

Employees agree that Loaded Dice may require remote wiping of work-related data from a personal device in cases of loss, theft, breach, or upon termination of employment.

### 4.2 Devices and support

- Devices must be presented for provisioning and configuration before connecting to Loaded Dice systems.
- Employees are responsible for the cost, maintenance, and repair of personal devices unless otherwise agreed.
- IT support for personal devices will be limited to installation of required apps and security tools.

### 4.3 Subject access requests

If a data subject submits an access request under GDPR, Loaded Dice may need to retrieve relevant information stored on a personal device. Employees must cooperate with such requests. Searches will be limited to the necessary data only.

### 4.4 Retention and deletion of data

- Work-related personal data must not be stored longer than necessary.
- Deleted data must be permanently erased, not simply moved to a recycle or trash folder.
- If removable media (e.g. USB drives, CDs) is used, data must be deleted once transfer is complete.

### 4.5 End of employment

Before leaving the company, employees must:

- Permanently delete all work-related data from their devices.
- Allow verification by IT/DPO that deletion has been carried out.

### 4.6 Third-party use

Employees must not allow friends, family, or other third parties to access Loaded Dice information on their devices. All Loaded Dice-related apps and data must remain password-protected.

## 5. Monitoring

Loaded Dice may monitor compliance with this policy, including the security of devices accessing Loaded Dice data.

Monitoring will be:

- Proportionate and for legitimate business purposes only.
- Limited to what is necessary for compliance, security, or legal reasons.
- Undertaken with employees' knowledge and consent.

Employees consent to such monitoring as a condition of connecting personal devices to Loaded Dice systems.

## 6. Non-compliance

Breaches of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal. In serious cases, employees may also be personally liable for breaches under data protection law.

## 7. Review and training

- Loaded Dice will provide mandatory data protection and IT security training to all employees on a regular basis.
- This policy will be reviewed annually or following significant legal, regulatory, or technological changes.
