## 1. Introduction

Loaded Dice is committed to protecting the privacy and rights of its employees in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the EU GDPR (where applicable), the Data Protection Act 2018, and all relevant laws.

This policy explains how we process personal data of employees, workers, contractors, and other individuals engaged by the Company. It applies to both automated and manual records and covers all stages of the employment relationship.

## 2. Data protection principles

Loaded Dice will comply with the following GDPR principles when processing personal data:

1. Process data lawfully, fairly, and transparently.
2. Collect data only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes.
3. Limit data to what is necessary for those purposes.
4. Keep data accurate and up to date.
5. Retain data only as long as necessary.
6. Protect data with appropriate security measures.
7. Be able to demonstrate compliance ("accountability").

## 3. Employee rights

Employees (as "data subjects") have the following rights:

- To be informed about the collection and use of their personal data.
- To access their data.
- To request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- To request deletion in certain circumstances.
- To restrict or object to processing in certain circumstances.
- To request portability of their data.
- To not be subject to solely automated decisions that have legal or significant effects.

Employees can exercise these rights by contacting the Data Protection Officer (DPO) or Data Protection Lead.

## 4. Lawful, fair, and transparent processing

Loaded Dice processes personal data only where a lawful basis applies. For most HR data this will be:

- Contractual necessity (e.g. payroll, benefits).
- Legal obligations (e.g. tax, health and safety).
- Legitimate interests (e.g. business operations, workforce planning).
- Consent (only where freely given and not a condition of employment).

Special category data (e.g. health, ethnicity) will only be processed under strict conditions permitted by law.

## 5. Data accuracy

Employees must ensure Loaded Dice holds accurate and up-to-date information. Personal details should be updated promptly via Leigh O'Dell, who handles HR. Loaded Dice will review employee data at least annually to maintain accuracy.

## 6. Data retention

Personal data will only be kept for as long as necessary for employment, legal, or business purposes. Typical retention periods include:

| Record type | Retention period |
|-------------|------------------|
| Payroll/tax records | 7 years |
| Recruitment data | 6 months (unless extended with consent) |
| Disciplinary records | 2 years |
| Health and safety records | As legally required |

A full retention schedule is available in the Loaded Dice Records Retention Policy.

## 7. Accountability and governance

Loaded Dice maintains records of processing activities, trains staff on data protection, and reviews compliance regularly. Where required, a Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed. If a formal DPO is not required, a Data Protection Lead will oversee compliance.

## 8. Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)

A DPIA will be carried out for any new project or system that involves high-risk processing (e.g. monitoring tools, large-scale health data). The DPO/Lead must be consulted before implementation.

## 9. Information for employees

Loaded Dice provides employees with a clear Privacy Notice that sets out:

- What data is collected.
- Why it is processed.
- The legal basis for processing.
- How long it is retained.
- Who it is shared with.
- Contact details of the DPO/Lead.

## 10. Categories of data processed

Loaded Dice processes the following types of employee data:

- **Personal identification:** name, date of birth, address, contact details, NI number.
- **Employment details:** job title, terms of employment, attendance, performance records.
- **Payroll and financial data:** salary, bank details, tax information.
- **Special category data:** health information, equal opportunities monitoring data (anonymised where possible).
- Disciplinary and grievance records.
- Training and development records.

## 11. Data security

**Technical measures**

- Secure servers, firewalls, and encryption (AES-256 or equivalent).
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for remote access and critical systems.
- Regular security patching and monitoring.
- Encrypted backups stored securely and tested monthly.

**Organisational measures**

- Access to employee data is limited to staff who need it for legitimate business purposes.
- All staff receive data protection training during onboarding and annual refreshers.
- Physical records are stored securely and disposed of via shredding/confidential waste.
- Remote working must comply with IT security standards (e.g. VPN, company-approved devices).

## 12. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the UK/EU, Loaded Dice will ensure appropriate safeguards, such as:

- Adequacy decisions (e.g. UK/EU recognised countries).
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
- EU–US Data Privacy Framework (if applicable).

## 13. Data breach notification

Employees must immediately report any suspected personal data breach (e.g. lost devices, mis-sent emails) to the DPO/IT.

Loaded Dice will:

- Assess and contain the breach.
- Notify the ICO within 72 hours if legally required.
- Inform affected employees where there is a high risk to their rights and freedoms.

## 14. Implementation and review

This policy is effective from 22 September 2025 and applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties handling employee data. It will be reviewed annually, or sooner if required by changes in law or business practices.
