## 1. Introduction

Shared Parental Leave (SPL) is available to eligible parents with babies due on or after 5 April 2015, or children placed for adoption on or after that date. SPL enables parents to share leave and pay in the first year after birth or adoption.

This policy applies to all employees of Loaded Dice, whether they are the mother/adopter or the partner. Each parent must liaise with their own employer to make SPL arrangements.

Employees should contact the HR Manager (Leigh O'Dell) for guidance before submitting a request, as SPL provisions are complex.

## 2. Aims of the policy

**2.1** To provide eligible employees with flexibility in caring for their child in the first year.

**2.2** To ensure the Company complies with its legal obligations in respect of SPL.

## 3. Eligibility and provision

### 3.1 Who can take SPL

SPL can be used only by:

- the mother/adopter, and
- either the child's father, or the spouse, civil partner, or partner of the mother/adopter.

Both parents must share the main responsibility for the child at birth/placement.

### 3.2 Employee criteria

The employee must:

- have 26 weeks' continuous service by the 15th week before the expected due date/matching date;
- still be employed at the start of each SPL period;
- be entitled (or linked to a partner entitled) to maternity/adoption leave or pay;
- have complied with maternity/adoption curtailment and SPL notice requirements; and
- have a partner who meets the statutory "employment and earnings test."

## 4. SPL entitlement

**4.1** Eligible employees may share up to 50 weeks' leave, depending on the maternity/adoption leave curtailed.

**4.2** SPL must be taken in full weeks, in either:

- a continuous block (which Loaded Dice must accept if eligibility and notice rules are met); or
- discontinuous blocks (subject to Loaded Dice agreement).

**4.3** Employees may submit up to three leave notifications. If a discontinuous pattern is refused, the employee may withdraw the request within 15 days or convert it to a continuous block.

**4.4** SPL must end within one year of birth/placement. Unused entitlement is lost.

## 5. Notice requirements

**5.1** Employees must give written notice to the HR Manager at least eight weeks before the intended start of SPL.

**5.2** Notice must include:

- the employee's name;
- the other parent's name;
- details of maternity/adoption leave or pay taken;
- the expected week of childbirth/matching date and actual date of birth/placement;
- the total SPL available and the intended division;
- a non-binding indication of the employee's proposed leave dates.

**5.3** Employees must also provide a signed declaration confirming eligibility and accuracy of information, and (if not the mother/adopter) their parental relationship.

**5.4** A signed partner declaration must also be provided, confirming eligibility, consent to the leave, and consent to Loaded Dice processing of personal data.

## 6. Evidence of eligibility

Within 14 days of notification, Loaded Dice may request:

- the name and address of the partner's employer; and/or
- the child's birth certificate (or adoption documents).

Employees must provide this within 14 days of request.

## 7. Booking SPL

**7.1** Notice of entitlement should usually be accompanied by a leave booking request.

**7.2** Employees may submit up to three requests, each specifying continuous or discontinuous leave.

**7.3** Loaded Dice will confirm acceptance or decision within 14 days of receipt. Discontinuous requests will be considered on a case-by-case basis, balancing employee needs and business impact.

## 8. Shared Parental Pay (ShPP)

### 8.1 Statutory entitlement

Eligible employees may share up to 37 weeks of statutory ShPP, depending on maternity/adoption pay already taken. ShPP is paid at the statutory rate set by the Government, or 90% of average weekly earnings if lower.

### 8.2 Eligibility for ShPP

The employee must:

- meet SPL eligibility;
- have average weekly earnings above the lower NI threshold;
- remain employed until the start of ShPP; and
- give eight weeks' written notice to the HR Manager.

Partner declarations must confirm consent to ShPP being claimed and, where applicable, that maternity/adoption pay has been curtailed.

## 9. Terms and conditions during SPL

All contractual terms, except salary, continue during SPL. Annual leave and pension contributions accrue in line with legislation and Company policy.

## 10. SPLIT days

Employees may work or attend training for up to 20 "Shared Parental Leave in Touch" (SPLIT) days without ending SPL or affecting ShPP. Payment arrangements for SPLIT days will be agreed in advance with the HR Manager.

## 11. Return to work

**11.1** Employees are expected to return on the next working day after the agreed end of SPL unless they notify Loaded Dice otherwise.

**11.2** Where the total leave taken (including maternity, paternity, adoption, and SPL) is 26 weeks or less, the employee has the right to return to the same job.

**11.3** If the total leave exceeds 26 weeks, or includes additional maternity or parental leave, the employee will normally return to the same job. If this is not reasonably practicable, they will return to a suitable alternative role on terms no less favourable.
