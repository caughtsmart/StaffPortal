Every employee signs an acknowledgment form confirming they have received and read the handbook. The wording of that form is reproduced below.

> I acknowledge that I have received, read, and understand the Loaded Dice Employee Policies and Procedures handbook.
>
> I understand that it is my responsibility to comply with the policies, rules, and guidelines outlined in the handbook, as well as any revisions or updates that may be issued in the future.
>
> I further acknowledge and agree that:
>
> 1. The policies described in the handbook are not intended to create, nor should they be construed as creating, a contract of employment.
> 2. My employment with Loaded Dice is at-will unless otherwise defined by applicable law or a written agreement signed by both Loaded Dice and myself. This means either I or Loaded Dice may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice.
> 3. If I have questions about any policy, I will seek clarification from my supervisor or the Human Resources manager.

The signed form records the employee's name, signature and date, and is countersigned by a manager. Completed forms are held on the employee's personnel file.
