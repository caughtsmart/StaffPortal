## Respect and inclusion

Every employee has the right to a safe, welcoming, and inclusive workplace where individuality is valued, and discrimination or harassment has no place.

## Fair pay and security

Employees have the right to fair wages in line with UK law, transparent contracts, and reliable scheduling that respects their time and commitments.

## Growth and development

Everyone has the right to training, feedback, and opportunities to grow their skills — whether in customer service, gaming knowledge, or creative hobbies.

## Work–life balance

All employees have the right to rest, breaks, and time away from work, ensuring a healthy balance between their passion for games and their personal wellbeing.
