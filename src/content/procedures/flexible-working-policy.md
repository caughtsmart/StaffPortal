## 1. Purpose

Loaded Dice recognises that flexible working can benefit both employees and the business by improving work–life balance, increasing engagement, and supporting diversity and inclusion. This policy sets out the rights of employees to request flexible working and how Loaded Dice will handle such requests in line with UK legislation.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees of Loaded Dice, regardless of role or seniority, who meet the statutory requirements to make a flexible working request.

## 3. Right to request flexible working

**3.1** From 6 April 2024, all employees have the statutory right to request flexible working from the first day of employment.

**3.2** Employees may make up to two statutory requests for flexible working within any 12-month period.

## 4. Types of flexible working

Examples of flexible working arrangements include (but are not limited to):

- Part-time working
- Job sharing
- Flexi-time
- Compressed hours
- Annualised hours
- Staggered hours
- Hybrid or remote working

## 5. Making a request

**5.1** Requests must be submitted in writing to the Managing Director, clearly stating:

- The change requested and proposed start date
- Whether a previous request has been made within the last 12 months
- The effect the employee thinks the change may have on the business and how this could be managed

**5.2** Loaded Dice may also accept informal requests for flexible working outside of the statutory process where appropriate.

## 6. Consideration of requests

**6.1** Loaded Dice will deal with all requests fairly, consistently, and promptly. A decision will normally be given within two months of receiving the request (unless extended by mutual agreement).

**6.2** A meeting may be held with the employee to discuss the request in detail. Employees may be accompanied at this meeting by a colleague or trade union representative.

**6.3** Requests will be assessed based on business needs, taking into account factors such as:

- The burden of additional costs
- The ability to meet customer demand
- Impact on quality, performance, or productivity
- Ability to reorganise work among staff or recruit additional employees

## 7. Outcome

**7.1** If the request is agreed, the change will be confirmed in writing and form a permanent variation to the employee's contract, unless otherwise agreed (e.g. a trial period).

**7.2** If the request is refused, Loaded Dice will provide a written explanation citing one of the statutory business grounds for refusal.

**7.3** Employees may appeal a decision by submitting an appeal in writing within 14 days of the decision.

## 8. Equality and diversity

Loaded Dice is committed to ensuring that flexible working requests are considered without discrimination and in line with the Equality Act 2010.

## 9. Monitoring and review

Loaded Dice will monitor the application of this policy and review it regularly to ensure compliance with UK law and alignment with business needs.
