## 1. Introduction

Loaded Dice is committed to providing a safe, healthy, and supportive working environment. This policy sets out our approach to drug use and misuse, helping us to protect the health, safety, and welfare of all employees, and to comply with our legal duties.

Legal framework:

- Section 2(2)(e) of the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974 requires employers to provide a safe and healthy working environment.
- The Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 makes it an offence to supply, produce, or permit the supply or use of controlled drugs on premises.
- It is also an offence to aid or abet any of these offences.

This policy applies to all employees and anyone working for or on behalf of Loaded Dice, including contractors, consultants, agency staff, and volunteers.

## 2. Aims of the policy

This policy aims to:

- Comply with legal requirements.
- Promote awareness of the risks and symptoms of drug misuse.
- Ensure employees understand their responsibilities regarding drugs and workplace safety.
- Support employees who seek help for drug-related problems, ensuring fair and consistent treatment.
- Safeguard all employees by ensuring a drug-free workplace.

## 3. Health and safety

Being under the influence of drugs at work can endanger the individual and others. Drug misuse may result in reduced attendance, lower efficiency and performance, impaired judgement, and increased accidents.

Possible signs of misuse may include:

- Noticeable or unexplained changes in behaviour;
- Confusion or disorientation;
- Irritability or mood swings;
- Fluctuations in energy levels;
- Decline in performance;
- Increased short-term sickness absence.

Employees who are concerned about a colleague are encouraged to:

- Urge the colleague to seek help; and/or
- Raise the matter confidentially with their line manager or HR.

Concerns must not be discussed with other colleagues.

Employees taking prescribed medication must seek medical advice about any impact on their ability to work safely and inform HR or their line manager if adjustments are needed.

## 4. Searches

Loaded Dice reserves the right to conduct searches for drugs on Loaded Dice premises, including bags, lockers, and cabinets.

Searches will be conducted respectfully and, where possible, in the presence of a witness. Employees may request a colleague to be present.

## 5. Prohibition

- Employees must be fit for work and able to perform their duties safely and effectively.
- Employees must not be under the influence of drugs or substances (including psychoactive substances, formerly known as "legal highs").
- Controlled or illegal substances must not be consumed, possessed, or brought onto Loaded Dice premises, land, or vehicles at any time.
- This prohibition applies to all work-related activities, including driving for business, on-call duties, business trips, training, or work-related social events.

## 6. Disciplinary action

Breaches of this policy may be treated as gross misconduct and could result in disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.

However, if an employee voluntarily declares a drug-related problem and agrees to undertake treatment or rehabilitation, Loaded Dice may suspend disciplinary action while treatment is pursued. Each case will be considered individually, balancing safety requirements with the need to support employees.

## 7. Principles

- All information about an employee's health, including drug-related matters, will be handled in accordance with the Loaded Dice Data Protection Policy.
- A drug-related problem will be treated as a health matter wherever possible, without excusing related misconduct or poor performance.
- Employees will be supported sympathetically and constructively and signposted to sources of further help.
- Requests for help will be treated in strict confidence, although some limited information-sharing may be necessary to provide support.
- Loaded Dice may, where appropriate, temporarily adjust duties or grant paid leave to support treatment.
- Following successful treatment, employees will normally be returned to their previous role or a suitable alternative.
- In cases of relapse, further support may be offered at Loaded Dice discretion. Repeated breaches of this policy may result in dismissal.
- If it is identified that the working environment contributes to drug-related problems, Loaded Dice will take reasonable steps to address those factors.

## 8. Responsibilities

**Managers are responsible for:**

- Ensuring all staff are aware of this policy and the standards expected.
- Taking action when behaviour falls below requirements.
- Supporting staff who seek help, in line with this policy.

**Employees are responsible for:**

- Ensuring they are fit for work.
- Seeking help if they experience drug-related issues.
- Supporting colleagues appropriately by raising concerns through the correct channels.
