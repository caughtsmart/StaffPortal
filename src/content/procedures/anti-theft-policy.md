## 1. Purpose

Loaded Dice is committed to protecting its people, property, assets, and reputation. This Anti-Theft Policy sets out the standards of behaviour expected of all employees, contractors, and visitors to ensure Loaded Dice property and customer property are safeguarded against theft, loss, or misuse.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees, whether permanent, temporary, or agency.
- Contractors, suppliers, and consultants working on Loaded Dice premises.
- Visitors granted access to Loaded Dice premises.

## 3. Policy statement

Loaded Dice has a zero-tolerance approach to theft. Any act of theft, attempted theft, or aiding theft — whether involving Loaded Dice property, customer property, or the belongings of colleagues — will be treated as gross misconduct and may result in disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal, and referral to the police.

## 4. Responsibilities

**Employees must:**

- Safeguard Loaded Dice assets and resources.
- Report any suspected theft, suspicious behaviour, or security breach immediately.
- Keep personal belongings secure.

**Managers must:**

- Ensure employees are aware of this policy.
- Investigate reports of theft promptly and fairly.

**Loaded Dice will:**

- Maintain security measures (e.g. CCTV, access controls, locks, alarm systems).
- Cooperate fully with law enforcement where theft is suspected.

## 5. Loaded Dice property

- All tools, equipment, stock, materials, documents, IT hardware, and intellectual property belong to Loaded Dice and must not be removed without prior authorisation.
- Misuse, unauthorised possession, or removal of Loaded Dice property will be treated as theft.

## 6. Searches

Where reasonable grounds exist, Loaded Dice reserves the right to:

- Conduct searches of employee personal property (bags, lockers, vehicles on Loaded Dice premises).
- Review CCTV footage in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Refusal to comply with a reasonable search request may itself be treated as a disciplinary matter.

## 7. Reporting

- Any suspected theft must be reported immediately to a line manager or the HR manager.
- Reports will be handled confidentially, and retaliation against whistleblowers will not be tolerated.

## 8. Disciplinary action

- Proven theft will normally be classed as gross misconduct, leading to summary dismissal.
- Loaded Dice reserves the right to involve the police and pursue criminal or civil action.

## 9. Review of policy

This policy will be reviewed annually to ensure it remains compliant with UK law and reflects best practice.
