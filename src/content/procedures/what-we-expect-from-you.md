## Positivity and respect

Treat every customer and teammate with kindness, patience, and inclusivity — helping us make the shop a welcoming space for all.

## Share your passion

Use your enthusiasm for games and hobbies to inspire others, offer helpful advice, and contribute to a fun, knowledgeable environment.

## Take pride in your work

Be reliable, professional, and attentive — whether running events, keeping the store organised, or supporting fellow staff.
