## 1. Introduction

This policy is designed to assist Loaded Dice in effectively managing sickness-related and other staff absence. Loaded Dice values supporting employees during sickness and absences and their subsequent return to work. Through an effective Sickness and Absence Policy, Loaded Dice will be better positioned to identify any potentially unsafe work practices, any issues affecting employee morale and any other underlying problems employees may be facing.

Any and all information provided by employees to Loaded Dice about their health (including, but not limited to, forms and medical records) will be collected, held, and processed lawfully and in accordance with Loaded Dice Data Protection Policy. Loaded Dice recognises that such data is sensitive and will handle it accordingly and in accordance with employees' rights under the law.

## 2. Notification of sickness

**2.1** If an employee is unable to come to work for any reason, they must inform Loaded Dice by contacting the Assistant Managing Director as soon as reasonably possible.

**2.2** Employees should speak to the Assistant Managing Director personally — i.e. calls on the employee's behalf from a friend, partner or parent will only be acceptable in exceptional circumstances. Employees may contact by email or text if they are unable to reach the Assistant Managing Director due to extenuating circumstances such as illness or emergency.

**2.3** The employee should indicate the reason for their absence, its likely duration and when the illness started.

**2.4** In the event that the employee's absence continues for a number of days or weeks, they must maintain regular contact with the Assistant Managing Director to keep Loaded Dice informed of the reasons for their ongoing absence and the date when they expect to be able to return to work. In such cases the employee should specify how the Assistant Managing Director can contact them if necessary, ideally leaving a landline number on which they can be contacted.

## 3. Certification of sickness

**3.1** All periods of absence through sickness must be certified by the statutory Form SC2. The completed form should indicate actual days of sickness, even if they include days when the employee would not normally have worked (e.g. weekends and public holidays).

**3.2** For sickness absences of up to 5 calendar days, the self-certification form should be completed by the employee upon their return to work and handed to the Assistant Managing Director.

**3.3** Employees are required to submit a medical certificate — the statutory Form Med 3, also referred to as a 'fit note' — within 7 days for absences lasting longer than 5 days. This will provide us with more information about your condition, and let us know whether your GP or medical provider considers that you are not 'fit for work', or 'may be fit for work taking account of the following advice'. Subsequent medical certificates must be produced as necessary to cover the total duration of the period of absence. As a minimum, employees should contact their manager on a weekly basis to provide an update on the injury or illness.

## 4. Long-term and persistent absence

**4.1** Loaded Dice are committed to working with employees during long-term illness and will explore all reasonable adjustments to help facilitate their return to work. Loaded Dice will treat as long-term absence any period of extensive absence due to serious or significant illness over a prolonged period. Persistent absence may consist of a series of unconnected short-term illnesses. Where Loaded Dice is of the opinion that a period of absence is long-term, it will inform the employee of such and:

- **4.1.1** require that the employee keep in regular contact with Loaded Dice, at such intervals as agreed between the Assistant Managing Director and the employee; and
- **4.1.2** ensure that the employee is kept informed as to any possible threat to their employment.

**4.2** Loaded Dice reserves the right to request a home visit where the illness is long-term. The purpose of the visit will be to discuss possibilities for a return to work and to discover whether Loaded Dice can assist in facilitating this. This visit will be arranged only if necessary and after other options (such as video calls) have been considered.

**4.3** Loaded Dice will treat as persistent absence a series of unconnected short-term illnesses.

**4.4** It may be necessary in incidences of long-term or persistent absence to treat the matter as an issue of capability or conduct. In such circumstances Loaded Dice will:

- **4.4.1** investigate the absence through "Return to Work Interviews" and the obtaining of medical reports;
- **4.4.2** set time limits on the assessment of the employee and keep him or her informed of such;
- **4.4.3** consider adjustments to the job in order to facilitate a return to work or to allow the employee to do their job more easily, for example the implementation of flexible working arrangements;
- **4.4.4** consider whether the illness amounts to a disability. Where it is found to do so the employee shall fall under the scope of the Loaded Dice Equal Opportunities and Diversity Policy and Loaded Dice shall make such reasonable adjustments as are necessary; and
- **4.4.5** keep the employee informed in all the circumstances of any threat to their employment.

**4.5** Where Loaded Dice requires medical reports relating to an absence it will either:

- **4.5.1** request that the employee undergo an independent medical examination; or
- **4.5.2** obtain a report from the employee's doctor and/or the Fit for Work assessment service, subject to employee consent.
- **4.5.3** Loaded Dice will make every effort to discuss any medical concerns with the employee before requesting an independent medical examination. Employees will be informed in advance of any such request.

In either case the employee may refuse to attend or refuse to consent to the release of a medical report, or request that corrections are made. Employees are reminded however that any decision regarding their future which could result in dismissal will be taken on the basis of the information available to Loaded Dice.

**4.6** Loaded Dice will hold all medical reports and related information obtained under Clause 4.5 as private and confidential and all such information will be handled in accordance with the Loaded Dice Data Protection Policy.

**4.7** Loaded Dice stresses that dismissal will only ever be taken as a last resort. Where however the absence is found to be a matter of misconduct, the employee will be subject to Loaded Dice disciplinary policy.

## 5. Sickness which occurs whilst the employee is on holiday

**5.1** Where an employee is incapacitated through sickness or injury during any period of pre-booked holiday (whether in whole or in part), Loaded Dice will, subject to the correct notification and certification, allow the employee to transfer to sick leave and take replacement holiday at a later date. This policy is subject to the following conditions, which will be strictly applied:

- **5.1.1** The total period of incapacity must be fully certificated by a qualified medical practitioner where it exceeds 7 days;
- **5.1.2** The employee must contact the Assistant Managing Director or the Managing Director (in person and by telephone if possible) as soon as they know that there will be a period of incapacity during the holiday; and
- **5.1.3** The employee must confirm in writing to the Assistant Managing Director no later than 48 hours after returning to work how much of the holiday period was affected by sickness or injury and the amount of leave that the employee wishes to take at another time.

**5.2** Any requests for replacement holiday must be made in accordance with Loaded Dice holiday policy and the employee should try to take the replacement holiday in the holiday year in which it was accrued. Where this is not possible, Loaded Dice will allow the employee to carry forward the leave into the next holiday year.

**5.3** Loaded Dice may require the employee to take all or part of their replacement holiday on particular days to be specified by the Assistant Managing Director.

## 6. Return to work interviews

Loaded Dice shall decide, after any absence due to sickness, whether the employee is required to attend a return-to-work interview with the Assistant Managing Director in order to:

**6.1** ensure the employee's fitness to return to work.

**6.2** agree any necessary actions required to facilitate the employee's return to work.

**6.3** ensure the proper certificates (e.g. Fit Note or Fit for Work return-to-work plan) have been completed or obtained in respect of the entire period of absence; and

**6.4** discuss any problems that may exist.

Return-to-work interviews aim to support employees in their transition back to work, ensuring that the employee's wellbeing and any adjustments needed are discussed in a positive, open manner. At the return-to-work interview, employees may be set reasonable targets and time limits for an improvement in attendance. A failure to improve may result in disciplinary action.

## 7. Statutory Sick Pay (SSP)

**7.1** In order to be eligible for SSP, employees must be ill for four days or longer (this can include weekends and bank holidays) and must have average weekly earnings equal to or more than the lower earnings limit. Please see relevant government websites such as HMRC and GOV.UK for details of the current lower earnings limit.

**7.2** Employees must use the statutory Form SC2 to provide the Company with details of their illness.

**7.3** The present weekly SSP rate can be found on relevant government websites such as HMRC and GOV.UK.

**7.4** The Company will record all details of SSP payments made to employees using Statutory Form SSP2 in conjunction with legal requirements.

**7.5** Where the Company is not required to pay SSP or SSP comes to an end, the Company will provide the employee with Form SSP1 to support the employee's claim for Employment and Support Allowance.

## 8. Company Sick Pay (CSP)

**8.1** Loaded Dice sick pay is entirely discretionary and does not form part of an employee's contractual entitlement. Loaded Dice reserves the right to withhold or withdraw such payments at its sole discretion, and no precedent shall be established by any previous payment. Statutory entitlements will remain unaffected.

**8.2** To be considered for CSP, the employee must have completed at least 3 months continuous service with Loaded Dice and have complied with Clauses 2 and 3 above. Entitlement to CSP is determined according to the employee's length of continuous service, as follows:

| Length of continuous service | Company Sick Pay |
|------------------------------|------------------|
| First 3 months | Statutory Sick Pay only |
| 3 – 24 months | 1 week's full basic pay |
| 2 – 5 years | 1 week's full basic and 2 weeks' half basic pay |
| 5 years or more | 2 weeks' full basic and 4 weeks' half basic pay |

**8.3** Where Loaded Dice makes full payment in times of illness or injury, this includes any entitlement to Statutory Sick Pay (SSP). Where Loaded Dice pays half pay, SSP will be added but the total of company pay and SSP will not exceed normal full basic pay.

**8.4** The employee will forfeit entitlement to CSP if:

- **8.4.1** They fail to comply with notification and certification requirements.
- **8.4.2** They make or produce any misleading or untrue statement or document concerning their fitness to work.
- **8.4.3** Their incapacity has been caused by participation in dangerous sports or activities.
- **8.4.4** If the absence is determined to be due to misconduct.

**8.5** Employees will be notified in writing of their sick pay entitlement and any changes to their pay during a period of illness.

## 9. Other absences — jury service

**9.1** Any employee called for jury service should inform the Assistant Managing Director as soon as they are summoned.

**9.2** Employees called for jury service will not be paid by Loaded Dice for the period of their absence. Employees should instead claim all available allowances from the Court.

## 10. Public duties

**10.1** Loaded Dice is legally obliged to permit any employee time off to complete their public duties including, but not limited to, magistrate or school governor duties. The employee should inform the Assistant Managing Director of their duties, meetings or rotas as soon as possible in order to allow the Company time to plan for their absence.

**10.2** Loaded Dice will pay employees carrying out public duties their normal basic wage in respect of practical time off for such duties, in recognition of their contribution to the local community and the benefits that the Company derives from their added skills development.

## 11. Emergency, maternity, paternity, parental and adoption leave

**11.1** Emergency leave (time off for dependants), maternity, paternity, parental and adoption leave are all dealt with in their respective policy documents, available from the Assistant Managing Director.

**11.2** If employees are uncertain about any other type of absence they must ask for advice from the Assistant Managing Director. Other types of absence may be covered by separate company policies and procedures and/or by statutory rights. Unauthorised absence is likely to be treated as a disciplinary offence.

## 12. Trade union

The Company is legally obliged to allow employees time off to carry out their trade union duties. Any time taken off will be paid unless the carrying out of such duties occurs outside working hours.

## 13. Monitoring

**13.1** Loaded Dice will monitor and record levels of absence and reasons for absence in order to help identify abuse of this policy, which places additional stress on colleagues. Further, Loaded Dice will be better positioned to identify unsatisfactory work practices and to distinguish between different types of absence.

**13.2** Loaded Dice will obtain consent from each employee, either in their Terms and Conditions of employment, or on a separate consent form, to comply with the relevant Data Protection legislation and the Loaded Dice Data Protection Policy.

**13.3** All information gathered through absence monitoring under this Policy will be held and treated in confidence and will be collected, held, and processed lawfully and in accordance with the Loaded Dice Data Protection Policy.
