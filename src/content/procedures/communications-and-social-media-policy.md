> Version v1.0, issued 22 September 2025. Reviewed annually, or sooner if legislation changes.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy applies to all employees of Loaded Dice who use Company communication tools, computers, mobile devices, or systems (Facilities).

The purpose of this policy is to:

- Support effective business communication.
- Protect the Company's reputation and legal compliance.
- Provide clear guidance on acceptable and unacceptable use of Company Facilities.

Limited personal use of Facilities is permitted where it does not interfere with work duties, breach this policy, or damage the Company's interests.

This policy should be read alongside Loaded Dice other policies, including the Data Protection Policy, Equal Opportunities and Diversity Policy, and Harassment and Bullying Policy.

## 2. General principles

Employees must always use Facilities responsibly, professionally, and in line with their contractual duties. In particular, employees must:

- Protect confidential, restricted, or sensitive information and only share it with proper authority.
- Handle personal data in line with the Loaded Dice Data Protection Policy.
- Respect copyright, intellectual property, and applicable laws.
- Avoid committing Loaded Dice to agreements without authorisation.
- Remember that communications may be relied upon in legal or regulatory proceedings.

### Prohibited use (gross misconduct)

Employees must never view, transmit, download, or access:

- Offensive, pornographic, discriminatory, or harassing material.
- Illegal or criminal material, including pirated software or content.
- Confidential information they are not authorised to access.
- Websites or services that the Company has blocked.

## 3. Internet use

- Internet access is provided primarily for business use. Limited personal use is permitted if reasonable and not disruptive.
- Employees must not attempt unauthorised access to systems, databases, or restricted areas.
- The introduction of malware, spyware, or any malicious code is strictly prohibited.
- No unauthorised software may be downloaded or installed.
- Content that is illegal, offensive, or likely to damage Loaded Dice reputation must not be accessed.
- Requests for access to blocked sites must be approved by the Managing Director.

## 4. Social media use

### 4.1 General rules

Social media includes, but is not limited to, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

When using social media, whether for personal or business purposes, employees must:

- Act responsibly and professionally.
- Protect confidential information, proprietary data, and personal data.
- Respect intellectual property rights.
- Avoid content that could harm Loaded Dice reputation or breach other Loaded Dice policies.
- Understand that online communications may be used in legal or regulatory proceedings.

If in doubt, employees should seek guidance from the Assistant Managing Director before posting.

### 4.2 Personal use

- Personal use of social media during work should be limited to breaks and must not affect productivity.
- Employees must not give the impression they are speaking on behalf of Loaded Dice unless authorised.
- Where Loaded Dice is mentioned, employees should make clear that opinions expressed are their own.
- Loaded Dice email addresses must not be used to register for personal social media accounts.

### 4.3 Business use

- Only employees authorised by the Managing Director may use social media on behalf of Loaded Dice.
- Business-related interactions must be accurate, respectful, and aligned with Loaded Dice messaging.
- Social media contacts made in the course of business are Loaded Dice property.
- Training may be required before employees manage official Loaded Dice accounts.

### 4.4 Unacceptable use

Employees must not post or share:

- Defamatory, misleading, discriminatory, or harassing content.
- Confidential or commercially sensitive information.
- Personal data of colleagues, customers, or other third parties without authority.
- Company trademarks, logos, or branding without approval.

## 5. Email use

### 5.1 Business email

- All business communications must be conducted via Loaded Dice email (@loadeddice.uk).
- Emails must be professional, accurate, and respectful.
- Confidential information must not be shared without authority.
- Emails may be disclosed in court or regulatory proceedings.
- Best practice includes proofreading, checking attachments, and ensuring disclaimers are included.

### 5.2 Personal email

- Personal email accounts may be accessed only occasionally and where it does not interfere with work.
- Loaded Dice email must not be used for personal purposes.

## 6. Telephone and mobile use

- Loaded Dice telephones and mobiles are provided for business purposes. Limited essential personal calls are acceptable but must not be excessive.
- Employees' personal mobile use should not disrupt work. Phones must be kept on silent during working hours.
- Excessive personal use of Loaded Dice or personal devices during work hours may lead to disciplinary action.

## 7. Security

- Employees are responsible for protecting Loaded Dice systems and data.
- Passwords must be kept secure and changed regularly.
- Confidential documents must be locked away when not in use; screens must be locked when unattended.
- Loaded Dice devices must be secured, especially when travelling.
- Public Wi-Fi should only be used if secure and Company-approved.
- No unauthorised equipment may be connected to Loaded Dice systems.

## 8. Monitoring

- Loaded Dice may monitor use of Facilities, in line with applicable law, for legitimate business purposes including security, service quality, compliance, and prevention of misuse.
- Internet traffic and email logs are retained. Personal use may therefore be visible.
- Employees should avoid visiting sites or sending emails that could reveal sensitive personal information (e.g. political or religious beliefs).
- Monitoring will be proportionate and in line with GDPR and other legal requirements.

## 9. Recruitment

As part of recruitment, Loaded Dice may review publicly available online information. Any searches will comply with data protection and equal opportunities obligations.

## 10. Misuse and compliance

- Misuse of Loaded Dice Facilities will be dealt with under the Loaded Dice Disciplinary Policy and may, in serious cases, constitute gross misconduct or a criminal offence.
- Loaded Dice may investigate misuse and, where criminal activity is suspected, report matters to the police.
- Employees who are uncertain whether an action is acceptable should seek guidance before proceeding.

## 11. Policy compliance

By using Loaded Dice Facilities, employees agree to comply with this policy. Breaches may result in disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.
