## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Records Retention Policy is to ensure that Loaded Dice manages its business records responsibly, in compliance with applicable UK legislation, regulatory requirements, and best practice standards.

This policy:

- Sets out how long records are retained.
- Provides guidance for secure storage, access, and disposal.
- Ensures compliance with the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and other relevant laws.
- Balances operational needs with data minimisation and privacy obligations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All records (paper and electronic) created, received, and maintained by Loaded Dice in the course of business.
- All employees, contractors, and third parties who handle company records.

Records include but are not limited to:

- Personnel and HR files
- Financial and accounting records
- Contracts and agreements
- Client and customer data
- Corporate governance documents
- Health and safety records
- Emails and electronic communications

## 3. Policy statement

Loaded Dice will:

- Retain records for the minimum period required by law or business necessity.
- Not retain records longer than necessary.
- Regularly review records to ensure they are current, accurate, and lawfully held.
- Dispose of records securely and irretrievably once the retention period has expired.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

- **Board of Directors / Senior Management:** Overall accountability for compliance.
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO) / Information Governance Lead:** Ensures compliance with UK GDPR and advises on data minimisation.
- **Department Heads:** Ensure adherence to retention schedules in their business areas.
- **All Staff:** Follow this policy when creating, storing, and disposing of records.

## 5. Retention periods

Retention periods will follow statutory requirements and industry best practices. Examples include:

| Record type | Retention period | Legal/regulatory reference |
|-------------|------------------|----------------------------|
| Personnel files (general) | 6 years after employment ends | Limitation Act 1980 |
| Payroll, tax, and NI records | 6 years | HMRC requirements |
| Contracts and agreements | 6 years after termination | Limitation Act 1980 |
| Health and safety records (e.g. accident reports) | 3 years minimum | RIDDOR 2013 |
| Medical records (work-related exposure) | 40 years | COSHH 2002 |
| Accounting records | 6 years | Companies Act 2006 |
| VAT records | 6 years | HMRC requirements |
| Board minutes and statutory registers | Permanently | Companies Act 2006 |
| Marketing data (prospects, leads) | Until consent withdrawn or 2 years inactivity | UK GDPR (consent/legitimate interest) |

A detailed retention schedule will be maintained as an appendix to this policy.

## 6. Secure storage

- Physical records must be stored in locked, access-controlled areas.
- Electronic records must be stored on secure servers with access restricted to authorised users.
- Cloud storage providers must meet UK GDPR standards.

## 7. Disposal of records

- Paper records must be cross-shredded or disposed of via an approved secure destruction service.
- Electronic records must be securely deleted or destroyed so they cannot be reconstructed.
- A disposal log must be maintained for high-risk or sensitive records.

## 8. Data subject rights

Under the UK GDPR, individuals have rights to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of their personal data. Retention of personal data will always respect these rights, unless an overriding legal obligation requires otherwise.

## 9. Monitoring and review

This policy will be:

- Reviewed annually by the DPO.
- Updated in line with changes to UK law, regulations, or business needs.

## 10. Breach of policy

Failure to follow this policy may result in disciplinary action and could expose Loaded Dice to legal, financial, or reputational risks.
