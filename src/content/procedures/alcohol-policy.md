## 1. Introduction

Loaded Dice is committed to being a responsible employer and ensuring the health, safety, and welfare of all employees. Alcohol-related problems can affect an employee's wellbeing, safety, and performance, and may also impact colleagues, customers, and the wider workplace.

This policy sets out our approach to alcohol consumption, our expectations of employees, and the support available for those who may develop alcohol-related problems. It also helps ensure compliance with the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, which places a duty on employers to provide a safe working environment and to protect the health and safety of employees, visitors, and contractors.

## 2. Aims of the policy

This policy aims to:

- Promote awareness of alcohol-related risks and addiction.
- Encourage a sensible and responsible approach to alcohol consumption.
- Ensure compliance with legal and health and safety obligations.
- Set clear restrictions on alcohol use in the workplace.
- Protect employees from the risks associated with alcohol misuse.
- Provide support to employees who may be experiencing alcohol-related difficulties.
- Establish fair and consistent procedures for managing alcohol-related issues.

## 3. Health, safety and performance

While moderate drinking is accepted in many social settings, alcohol consumption in the workplace can:

- Impair performance and decision-making.
- Lead to inappropriate or unsafe behaviour.
- Place the individual and colleagues at risk.
- Have a negative impact on health over time.

Alcohol misuse in the workplace may take two forms:

- **Occasional inappropriate drinking** — often managed as misconduct.
- **Consistent or long-term misuse** — more likely to involve health and performance concerns.

Signs of possible alcohol misuse may include:

- Frequent or unexplained absences.
- Unusually high sickness levels, particularly around weekends.
- Decline in appearance or hygiene.
- Irregular work patterns or reduced productivity.
- Difficulty maintaining good working relationships.

These indicators do not, on their own, prove alcohol misuse, but concerns should be raised confidentially with a manager.

## 4. Restrictions on alcohol use

- Employees must not consume alcohol during working hours unless formal approval has been given by the Managing Director.
- Employees must not attend work or represent Loaded Dice while unfit for duty due to alcohol.
- Alcohol should not be consumed in excess while attending business functions, events, or hospitality on behalf of Loaded Dice.
- Employees are not required to work with anyone they reasonably believe is unfit due to alcohol. Concerns should be reported immediately to a manager.
- Employees who are unfit for duty because of alcohol will be removed from duty and the matter referred for further action.
- If an accident or incident occurs while an employee is under the influence of alcohol, the employee may be held personally responsible in addition to any Loaded Dice liability.

## 5. Support for employees

Loaded Dice recognises that alcohol dependency can be a medical condition and will treat it as a health matter where possible. Employees are encouraged to seek help at an early stage. Support may include:

- Confidential discussions with a manager or HR.
- Referral to occupational health or counselling services.
- Signposting to external specialist organisations.

All requests for support will be treated in the strictest confidence and any information gathered will be managed in line with the Loaded Dice Data Protection Policy.

## 6. Procedure

1. Concerns about alcohol misuse should be reported confidentially to the employee's line manager or HR.
2. The employee will be invited to a private meeting to discuss the concern.
3. Where appropriate, referral to occupational health or an external service may be arranged.
4. Medical advice may be sought to confirm whether an alcohol-related condition exists.
5. If the employee agrees to follow an appropriate treatment plan, disciplinary action may be paused while support is provided.
6. Adjustments may be considered, such as lighter duties or short periods of leave, where appropriate.
7. Following successful treatment, Loaded Dice will seek to return the employee to their role or, if not possible, an alternative suitable position.
8. In cases of repeated relapse, Loaded Dice may not be able to continue providing support, and disciplinary action up to and including dismissal may follow.

## 7. Disciplinary action

- Employees who attend work unfit through alcohol may be subject to disciplinary action under the Loaded Dice disciplinary policy.
- Being unfit for duty through alcohol may amount to gross misconduct, which could lead to summary dismissal.
- Disciplinary action may also apply if alcohol misuse leads to misconduct, unsafe behaviour, or damage to Loaded Dice reputation.

## 8. Responsibility for implementation

- Line managers are responsible for monitoring performance and behaviour, addressing concerns, and referring issues appropriately.
- The Assistant Managing Director will oversee policy implementation and ensure employees are treated fairly and consistently.
- Employees are responsible for ensuring they are fit for duty and for raising concerns about their own or others' fitness to work.

## 9. Review of policy

This policy will be reviewed every two years, or earlier if there are changes in legislation or Loaded Dice practice.
