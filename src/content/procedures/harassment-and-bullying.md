## 1. Policy statement

**1.1** Loaded Dice is committed to providing a safe, supportive, and stimulating working environment in which all staff can achieve their potential. Such an environment cannot exist where any individual is subjected to harassment, bullying, intimidation, coercion, or other inappropriate conduct.

**1.2** The organisation is fully committed to the principles of equality, diversity, and inclusion. Harassment and bullying are recognised as forms of discrimination and will not be tolerated under any circumstances.

**1.3** All complaints of harassment or bullying will be treated seriously, investigated promptly, and handled with sensitivity and confidentiality.

**1.4** This Policy applies to all employees, managers, contractors, agency workers, interns, consultants, and third parties engaged by Loaded Dice. It covers conduct in the workplace, at work-related events, during business travel, and in any work-related online or digital communications.

## 2. Definitions of harassment and bullying

**2.1** Harassment is unwanted conduct which has the purpose or effect of violating a person's dignity, or creating an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating, or offensive environment. It may relate (but is not limited) to a person's sex, marital status, race, disability, religion or beliefs, sexual orientation, age, or gender reassignment.

**2.2** Bullying is offensive, intimidating, malicious, or insulting behaviour involving misuse of power that undermines, humiliates, or causes harm to another individual.

**2.3** Harassment and bullying may:

- Be persistent or occur as isolated incidents.
- Be obvious or subtle.
- Occur face-to-face, in writing, electronically (including emails, texts, and social media), or indirectly.
- Arise from unequal or unfair application of rules or monitoring.

**2.4** Examples include (but are not limited to):

- Spreading malicious rumours or gossip.
- Deliberate exclusion from professional or social activities.
- Verbal abuse, insults, threats, or offensive jokes.
- Unwelcome sexual advances, comments, or physical contact.
- Threats relating to job security.
- Undermining competence through unreasonable workload or unfair criticism.
- Overbearing supervision or excessive monitoring.
- Circulation of offensive materials, graffiti, or images.
- Online or digital harassment (including social media).
- Inciting others to engage in such behaviour.

## 3. Unlawful grounds of harassment or bullying

Loaded Dice will not tolerate harassment or bullying on any grounds. The following are expressly unlawful and prohibited under the Equality Act 2010 and other relevant legislation:

- **Sex:** harassment or bullying based on sex, pregnancy, or maternity.
- **Marital or civil partnership status.**
- **Gender reassignment:** harassment due to an individual's intent to undergo, undergoing, or having undergone gender reassignment.
- **Sexual harassment:** unwanted physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.
- **Race:** including colour, ethnicity, or nationality.
- **Disability:** relating to any physical or mental impairment.
- **Sexual orientation:** including heterosexual, gay, lesbian, bisexual, or other orientations.
- **Religion or belief:** including philosophical beliefs protected under law.
- **Age:** applicable to all ages.
- **Grievance:** victimisation for raising a grievance.

## 4. Reporting harassment or bullying

**4.1** Employees have the right to raise a complaint if they believe they are being bullied or harassed by colleagues, managers, or third parties (e.g. clients, suppliers, contractors).

**4.2** Employees who raise genuine complaints will not suffer detriment, victimisation, or retaliation.

**4.3** Complaints may be raised informally or formally, depending on the circumstances.

## 5. Procedure for complaints

**5.1** Complaints should be raised as soon as reasonably practicable.

**5.2** Employees may report concerns to their line manager, the Assistant Managing Director, the Managing Director, or Human Resources. Where the complaint involves one of these individuals, an alternative reporting route will be provided.

### 5.3 Informal procedure

- Employees are encouraged, where appropriate, to address the behaviour directly with the individual concerned, making clear that it is unwanted and must stop.
- Alternatively, employees may seek support from a manager, colleague, or HR representative to address the matter informally.
- Employees may also submit their concerns in writing, keeping copies of correspondence and maintaining an incident diary.
- If informal resolution is unsuccessful or inappropriate, a formal complaint may be submitted.

### 5.4 Formal procedure

A formal complaint should be raised under the Loaded Dice Grievance Procedure. It should include:

1. The name of the person whose behaviour is alleged to be harassment or bullying.
2. Details of the behaviour, including specific examples, dates, and times.
3. Names of any witnesses.
4. Steps already taken to address the matter.

Complaints will normally be acknowledged within five working days and investigated within 20 working days, subject to complexity. The person accused will be informed of the complaint and given an opportunity to respond.

## 6. Consequences of breach

**6.1** Harassment and bullying are disciplinary offences. Outcomes may include informal action, mandatory training, written warnings, or dismissal.

**6.2** Serious incidents may amount to gross misconduct, justifying summary dismissal without notice.

**6.3** Certain forms of harassment or bullying may also constitute a criminal offence, leading to prosecution, fines, or imprisonment.

## 7. Responsibilities of employees and managers

- All employees must treat others with dignity and respect and comply with this Policy.
- Managers must promote a culture of respect, ensure staff are aware of the Policy, and intervene promptly when unacceptable behaviour is observed or reported.
- Any complaints received must be handled fairly, consistently, and confidentially.
- All incidents will be reported to the Assistant Managing Director and HR.

## 8. Support and advice

Loaded Dice will offer counselling, mediation, or other appropriate support. Employees may also seek confidential advice from HR, trade union representatives, or external advisory services such as ACAS.

## 9. Confidentiality

**9.1** All complaints will be handled with strict confidentiality, consistent with a fair investigation process.

**9.2** Employees involved in an investigation must respect confidentiality at all times.

**9.3** Records of complaints and investigations will be kept securely and processed in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

**9.4** Confidentiality may be overridden where disclosure is legally required (e.g. safeguarding or criminal activity).

**9.5** Breaches of confidentiality may result in disciplinary action.
