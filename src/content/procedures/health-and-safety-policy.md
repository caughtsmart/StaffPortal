## 1. Introduction

Loaded Dice actively promotes a safe working environment for its employees and visitors/customers. Working with continuous improvement, Loaded Dice seeks to have the safest workspaces, while promoting health, safety and environmental initiatives with a dedicated workforce engaged at all levels.

## 2. Policy statement

### Part 1: Statement of intent

This is the health and safety policy statement of **Loaded Dice**.

Mr Graham Scutt, Managing Director.

- **Warehouse / Head Office:** Loaded Dice, Unit C4, Windmill Park, Hayes Road, Sully, Vale of Glamorgan, CF64 5AD
- **Retail Shop:** Loaded Dice, 28 Holton Road, Barry, Vale of Glamorgan, CF63 4HD

Our health and safety policy is to:

- prevent accidents and cases of work-related ill health
- manage health and safety risks in our workplace
- provide clear instructions and information, and adequate training, to ensure employees are safe and competent to do their work
- provide personal protective equipment
- consult with our employees on matters affecting their health and safety
- provide and maintain safe plant and equipment
- ensure safe handling and use of substances
- maintain safe and healthy working conditions
- implement emergency procedures, including evacuation in case of fire or significant incident
- review and revise this policy regularly

### Part 2: Responsibilities for health and safety

**1. Overall and final responsibility for health and safety:** Mr Graham Scutt, Managing Director.

**2. Day-to-day responsibility for ensuring this policy is put into practice:** Mr Christopher Scutt.

**3. To ensure health and safety standards are maintained and improved, the following people have responsibility in the following areas:**

- Mr Christopher Scutt — Warehouse and Shop
- Mr Ben Roberts — Shop

**4. All employees should, in line with the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974:**

- co-operate with supervisors and managers on health and safety matters;
- use and take care of PPE as directed by management and risk assessment;
- take reasonable care of their own health and safety; and
- report all health and safety concerns to an appropriate person.
