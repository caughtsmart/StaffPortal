## 1. Community first

We're more than just a shop — we're a hub where friendships are forged, rivalries are playful, and everyone feels welcome at the table.

## 2. Play without barriers

Hobbies and games are for everyone. We champion inclusivity, accessibility, and diversity so that all players feel at home, whether they're rolling their first dice or painting their hundredth miniature.

## 3. Passion for play

We share in the excitement of a well-played game, the joy of collecting, and the creativity of hobby craftsmanship. Our passion fuels everything we do.

## 4. Knowledge you can trust

From rules clarifications to hobby tips, we pride ourselves on giving honest, reliable advice to help you enjoy your games to the fullest.

## 5. Supporting local creativity

We showcase and support local makers, artists, and indie game creators, ensuring our shelves reflect the richness of the UK gaming community.

## 6. Sustainability matters

We aim to reduce waste, recycle wherever possible, and support eco-conscious brands so future generations can enjoy the worlds of imagination we love.

## 7. Fun above all

At the heart of every dice roll, card draw, or model build is joy. We exist to celebrate that fun, whether in-store, online, or at the gaming table.
