## 1. Policy statement

Loaded Dice is committed to creating and maintaining a working environment that promotes equality of opportunity, values diversity, and is free from discrimination, harassment, and victimisation.

We embrace the principles set out in the Equality Act 2010, ensuring that no employee, job applicant, client, or customer is treated less favourably on the basis of any protected characteristic, including:

- Age
- Disability
- Gender reassignment
- Marriage and civil partnership
- Pregnancy and maternity
- Race (including colour, nationality, and ethnic or national origin)
- Religion or belief
- Sex
- Sexual orientation

We are committed to removing barriers to participation, challenging unfair practices, and fostering a culture of dignity and respect for all.

All personal data processed in connection with this Policy will be handled in line with the Company's Data Protection Policy.

## 2. Objectives

The objectives of this Policy are to:

- Prevent and eliminate all forms of unlawful discrimination, harassment, and victimisation.
- Ensure equality of opportunity in recruitment, training, promotion, pay, benefits, and career development.
- Support an inclusive workplace culture in which individual differences are respected and valued.
- Provide services and goods to clients and customers in a way that is inclusive and free from discrimination.

## 3. Designated Officer

Loaded Dice employ a Designated Officer to oversee compliance with this Policy and to act as the first point of contact for concerns.

Currently: Assistant Managing Director. Contact details are available internally.

## 4. Definitions

Discrimination is unequal treatment based on a protected characteristic, resulting in a person being treated more or less favourably than others in comparable circumstances. It may be:

- **Direct discrimination** – where someone is treated less favourably because of a protected characteristic.
- **Indirect discrimination** – where a provision, criterion, or practice applies to everyone but disproportionately disadvantages people with a protected characteristic, and cannot be objectively justified.
- **Harassment** – unwanted conduct related to a protected characteristic that violates a person's dignity or creates an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating, or offensive environment.
- **Victimisation** – treating someone less favourably because they have raised or supported a complaint of discrimination.

## 5. Positive action

Under Section 159 of the Equality Act 2010, the Company may take steps to encourage applications from underrepresented groups.

In recruitment or promotion, positive action will only be applied in a tie-break situation, where candidates are equally qualified.

## 6. Reasonable adjustments

The Company has a duty to make reasonable adjustments to remove barriers faced by employees or applicants with disabilities. These may include (but are not limited to):

- Adjustments to premises or equipment
- Re-allocation of duties
- Flexible working arrangements
- Provision of training, mentoring, or adapted materials
- Paid time off for treatment or rehabilitation

Adjustments will be agreed in consultation with the individual concerned and implemented where reasonable and practicable.

## 7. Responsibilities

- All employees, contractors, and agents must avoid discriminatory behaviour and treat others with dignity and respect.
- Managers and senior leaders are responsible for promoting this Policy, ensuring fair decision-making, and addressing concerns promptly.
- Loaded Dice accepts ultimate responsibility for achieving the objectives of this Policy and ensuring compliance with equality law.

Employees may be held individually liable for discriminatory acts. In some cases, an Employment Tribunal may also order them to pay compensation.

## 8. Reporting and acting on concerns

Any employee who experiences, witnesses, or suspects discrimination, harassment, or victimisation should report it to the Designated Officer as soon as possible.

- All concerns will be treated seriously, investigated promptly, and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
- No employee will suffer retaliation or victimisation for raising a concern in good faith.

- **Internal support:** HR or employee/trade union representatives

  - Citizens Advice Bureau — [www.citizensadvice.org.uk](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk)
  - Civil Legal Advice (for free legal aid advice) — 0345 345 4 345 | [www.gov.uk/civil-legal-advice](https://www.gov.uk/civil-legal-advice)

## 10. Monitoring, review, and extent of the Policy

- This Policy applies to all aspects of employment, including recruitment, training, performance management, promotion, pay, benefits, redundancy, and dismissal. It also applies to the provision of goods and services.
- The Policy does not form part of an employee's contract of employment.
- Loaded Dice reserves the right to amend this Policy when necessary.
- This Policy will be reviewed annually to ensure it remains up to date, relevant, and effective.
