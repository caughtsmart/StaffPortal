## 1. Introduction

**1.1** It is the policy of Loaded Dice to ensure that, as far as possible, employees are able to balance their career and family responsibilities. Loaded Dice recognises that parenthood brings additional responsibilities and is committed to supporting employees throughout this period.

**1.2** This policy sets out the statutory rights and responsibilities of employees who are pregnant or have recently given birth. Loaded Dice implements maternity rights as set out in current legislation.

**1.3** The following abbreviations are used in this policy:

- **EWC** – Expected Week of Childbirth: the week, starting on a Sunday, in which a doctor or midwife expects the employee to give birth.
- **QW** – Qualifying Week: the 15th week before the EWC.

**1.4** Employees and their partners may also be eligible for Shared Parental Leave (SPL), which enables parents to share leave and pay in the first year. Employees must take at least two weeks of maternity leave immediately following the birth before opting into SPL. For more details, see the Company's Shared Parental Leave Policy.

**1.5** This policy applies to employees only. It does not apply to agency workers or self-employed contractors. The policy does not form part of any employee's contract of employment and may be amended by Loaded Dice at any time.

## 2. Time off for antenatal care

**2.1** Once an employee has notified their line manager of their pregnancy, they are entitled to reasonable paid time off to attend antenatal appointments recommended by a doctor, midwife or health visitor. Antenatal care may include relaxation and parentcraft classes.

**2.2** This right applies regardless of the employee's length of service.

**2.3** Employees who are the father of the baby or the partner of the pregnant employee are entitled to unpaid time off to attend up to two antenatal appointments (maximum 6.5 hours each).

## 3. Entitlement to maternity leave

All employees are entitled to up to 52 weeks' maternity leave, consisting of:

- 26 weeks' Ordinary Maternity Leave (OML), and
- 26 weeks' Additional Maternity Leave (AML).

## 4. Notification of pregnancy

**4.1** Employees should notify their line manager of their pregnancy as soon as possible so that Loaded Dice can carry out any necessary health and safety risk assessments.

**4.2** By the end of the Qualifying Week (QW), or as soon as reasonably practicable afterwards, the employee must provide the following in writing:

- confirmation of pregnancy,
- their Expected Week of Childbirth (EWC), and
- the date they intend to start maternity leave.

**4.3** The employee must also provide their MAT B1 form, signed by a doctor or midwife, confirming the EWC.

## 5. Maternity leave

**5.1** Maternity leave can start no earlier than 11 weeks before the EWC, unless the baby is born earlier.

**5.2** Leave will begin on the earliest of:

- the employee's chosen start date,
- the day after childbirth, or
- the day after absence from work for a pregnancy-related reason in the four weeks before the EWC.

**5.3** Employees may bring forward or postpone their chosen start date by giving at least 28 days' written notice (or as soon as reasonably practicable).

**5.4** Loaded Dice will confirm in writing the employee's maternity leave and expected return date within 28 days of receiving notification.

**5.5** Shortly before maternity leave starts, Loaded Dice will discuss handover arrangements and options for staying in touch.

**5.6** By law, all employees must take at least two weeks' compulsory maternity leave immediately after childbirth (four weeks for factory workers).

## 6. Maternity pay

**6.1** Employees with at least 26 weeks' continuous service at the end of the QW, and who meet the earnings threshold, are entitled to Statutory Maternity Pay (SMP) for up to 39 weeks.

**6.2** SMP is paid as follows:

- **First 6 weeks:** 90% of average weekly earnings,
- **Next 33 weeks:** at the government-set rate (or 90% of average weekly earnings if lower).

## 7. Rights during maternity leave

**7.1** During maternity leave, the employee's contract of employment remains in force, except for the right to remuneration.

**7.2** Employees will:

- retain all contractual rights (e.g. notice, redundancy pay, access to grievance and disciplinary procedures, and implied obligations of trust and confidence), and
- remain bound by contractual obligations (e.g. confidentiality, good faith, and restrictions on other employment).

**7.3** Statutory and contractual holiday entitlement continues to accrue during maternity leave.

**7.4** The timing of holiday should be agreed before maternity leave begins. Loaded Dice may:

- require employees to take outstanding holiday before leave,
- allow it to be carried forward, or
- pay in lieu of contractual holiday entitlement above the statutory minimum.

**7.5** If the employee is a member of a pension scheme, Loaded Dice will continue employer contributions during OML and any paid AML, based on normal salary. Employee contributions will be based on maternity pay unless the employee opts to maintain full contributions.

**7.6** Employees may, by agreement, work up to 10 "Keeping in Touch" (KIT) days during maternity leave. KIT days are paid at the normal basic rate, inclusive of maternity pay.

**7.7** Loaded Dice may make reasonable contact during maternity leave but will not require work to be undertaken.

## 8. Returning to work

**8.1** Employees are expected to return on their confirmed return date unless they request otherwise. Employees wishing to return earlier must give at least eight weeks' notice.

**8.2** Employees returning from OML are entitled to return to their original job on the same terms and conditions.

**8.3** Employees returning from AML are entitled to return to their original job, or if this is not reasonably practicable, to a suitable alternative role on no less favourable terms.

**8.4** Employees wishing to change working hours or arrangements on return from maternity leave may request this under the Company's Flexible Working Policy.

**8.5** Employees who decide not to return must resign in accordance with their contract of employment.

## 9. Shared parental leave

**9.1** Employees may be eligible to opt into Shared Parental Leave (SPL) once they have taken at least two weeks of maternity leave.

**9.2** To end maternity leave and opt into SPL, employees must give at least eight weeks' written notice. SPL allows the remaining maternity leave and pay to be shared with a partner, subject to eligibility.

## 10. Pregnancy loss

**10.1** In the event of a miscarriage before 24 weeks, employees will be supported through the Company's Sickness Absence Policy.

**10.2** In the event of a stillbirth or neonatal loss at or after 24 weeks, employees remain entitled to the full maternity leave and pay provisions outlined in this policy.
