## 1. Purpose

Loaded Dice is committed to acting with integrity and conducting business in an honest, transparent, and ethical way. We have a zero-tolerance approach to bribery and corruption and expect the same from everyone working with or for us.

This Policy helps ensure compliance with the Bribery Act 2010 and applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, business partners, agents, and anyone else representing the Company.

## 2. What is bribery?

Bribery is offering, giving, requesting, or receiving something of value to improperly influence a decision or action.

Examples include:

- Paying or accepting money, gifts, or favours to secure a contract.
- Offering excessive hospitality to gain an unfair business advantage.
- Requesting benefits in return for awarding work.

Bribery of foreign officials is also prohibited, unless expressly allowed by law.

## 3. Consequences of bribery

- **For individuals:** Breaches may result in disciplinary action (up to and including dismissal) and could lead to criminal prosecution, fines, and/or imprisonment of up to 10 years.
- **For the Company:** A breach could result in unlimited fines, reputational damage, and loss of business.

## 4. Responsibilities

Everyone working with or for Loaded Dice must:

- Avoid any activity that could be seen as bribery or corruption.
- Stay alert to potential risks, especially when working internationally or with third parties.
- Report any concerns immediately to the Managing Director.

## 5. Facilitation payments

Facilitation payments (small unofficial payments to speed up routine tasks) are bribes and strictly prohibited.

The only exception is where refusing a payment would put someone's life or safety at risk. Any such payment must be reported to the Managing Director as soon as possible.

## 6. Gifts and hospitality

Gifts and hospitality can support good business relationships but must be handled carefully. They are acceptable only if they are:

- Reasonable, proportionate, and infrequent.
- Not intended to influence a decision or secure business unfairly.
- Recorded in the Gifts & Hospitality Register.

The following rules apply:

- Cash gifts are never allowed.
- Do not give or accept gifts or hospitality during contract tenders or negotiations.
- If refusing a gift would cause significant cultural offence, it must be reported and donated to charity.

## 7. Charitable donations

- Donations may only be made to registered charities.
- All donations must be recorded and proof of receipt obtained.
- Donations must never be used to gain a business advantage.
- Cash donations are not permitted.

## 8. Political donations

The Company does not make political donations and is not affiliated with any political party or candidate.

Employees may make personal political contributions, but not on behalf of Loaded Dice and never to secure a business advantage.

## 9. Risk awareness and due diligence

Be especially vigilant when:

- Operating in countries with high levels of corruption.
- Being asked to use unusual payment methods (e.g. cash or intermediaries).
- Dealing with permits, licences, customs, or import/export matters.
- Working with new or unfamiliar third parties.

Always carry out due diligence and seek advice if unsure.

## 10. Reporting concerns

If you suspect or become aware of bribery or corruption, you must report it. Concerns can be raised with:

- the Managing Director, or
- the Assistant Managing Director.

Reports will be treated seriously, confidentially, and without retaliation.

## 11. Summary — what this means for you

- Do not give or accept bribes.
- Avoid anything that could appear improper.
- Report concerns immediately.
- When in doubt, ask before acting.
