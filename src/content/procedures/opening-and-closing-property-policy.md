## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the responsibilities and procedures for opening and closing company property to ensure the security of personnel, assets, and facilities.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and authorised personnel responsible for opening or closing any company-owned or leased facility.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Designated personnel:** Only employees specifically assigned and trained may open or close company property.
- **Supervisors/managers:** Ensure staff are trained in these procedures and update assignments as necessary.
- **Employees:** Must follow all procedures without deviation unless authorised by management.
- **Key/access holders:** Employees with access to company keys and alarm fobs must bring them to work each day and keep them secured at all times.

## 4. Opening procedures

**Arrival**

**4.1** Arrive at least 15 minutes prior to scheduled opening time.

**4.2** Ensure the area around the entrance is safe and clear of suspicious activity.

**Unlocking**

**4.3** Use assigned access credentials (key, fob, or code).

**4.4** Disarm the security system immediately upon entry.

**Initial check**

**4.5** Conduct a walkthrough of the premises to confirm no unauthorised entry or safety hazards.

**4.6** Report any irregularities to management immediately.

**Preparation for business**

**4.7** Turn on lights, HVAC, and other necessary equipment.

**4.8** Ensure entryways are unlocked for staff/customers at the scheduled time.

## 5. Closing procedures — end-of-day checks

**5.1** Confirm all staff and visitors have exited the premises.

**5.2** Secure sensitive materials (documents, cash, equipment) in designated storage.

## 6. Shut down

**6.1** Turn off non-essential equipment, lights, and appliances.

**6.2** Lock internal storage rooms, offices, or restricted areas as required.

## 7. Final lock-up

**7.1** Lock all exterior doors and windows.

**7.2** Arm the security system.

**7.3** Double-check perimeter security (gates, fences, or exterior locks).

## 8. Exit

**8.1** Leave the premises promptly after securing.

**8.2** Do not remain on-site after closing unless authorised by management.

## 9. Security and safety

**9.1** Keys, codes, or access cards must not be shared with unauthorised individuals.

**9.2** Employees must immediately report any lost, stolen, or forgotten keys or alarm fobs to management.

**9.3** Employees may be held responsible for replacement costs or security re-keying if keys, fobs, or access devices are lost due to negligence.

**9.4** Any incidents (break-ins, suspicious activity, system malfunctions) must be documented and reported to management and, if necessary, security or law enforcement.

## 10. Compliance

All employees are expected to comply with this policy. Failure to do so may result in:

- **Verbal or written warning** for minor or first-time violations.
- **Suspension or loss of access privileges** for repeated or serious violations.
- **Financial responsibility** for replacement costs or re-keying when loss occurs due to negligence.
- **Termination of employment** for wilful disregard of security procedures, repeated violations, or behaviour that places company property or personnel at risk.
