## 1. Policy statement

Loaded Dice recognises that its people are its most valuable asset. We are committed to supporting flexible ways of working that balance business needs with employees' wellbeing. Home working is one form of flexible working. It may be arranged on a regular or occasional basis, where appropriate to the role and subject to agreement with Loaded Dice. This policy sets out the principles, processes and responsibilities for home working.

## 2. Aims and benefits

This policy aims to:

- provide greater flexibility and choice in working arrangements;
- support employee wellbeing and work–life balance;
- improve recruitment and retention;
- maintain or enhance efficiency and service delivery;
- reduce environmental impact and optimise use of office space.

## 3. Definition of home working

- **Regular home working** – a formal agreement to work from home for all or part of the working week.
- **Occasional/ad hoc home working** – short-term or one-off arrangements agreed with the line manager, for example to complete specific tasks or manage exceptional circumstances.

The employee's contractual base remains the Loaded Dice office unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 4. Principles

- All employees may request home working, but not all roles are suitable.
- Requests will be considered fairly, consistently and in line with current flexible working legislation.
- Home working must support both business needs and employee needs.
- Employees working from home are expected to maintain regular contact with their line manager and remain available during agreed working hours.
- Loaded Dice treats home- and office-based staff equitably.

## 5. Requesting home working

- Requests should be made in writing to the line manager, setting out the reasons, proposed start date, and how any impact on the business can be managed.
- Loaded Dice will meet with the employee within 28 days of receiving a request and confirm the outcome in writing.
- Approved arrangements will be subject to a trial period before confirmation.
- Decisions will consider role requirements, service delivery, costs, and employee circumstances.
- Employees may appeal refusals in line with the Loaded Dice Flexible Working Policy.
- All approved home working arrangements require a signed Home Working Agreement.

## 6. Equipment and connectivity

- Loaded Dice will provide necessary IT equipment (e.g. laptop, headset, secure access to systems).
- Where business needs require additional equipment, this will be agreed in advance.
- Equipment remains Loaded Dice property and must be used only for business purposes.
- Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of Loaded Dice equipment and returning it when requested.
- Use of personal equipment may be permitted if it meets Loaded Dice security and health and safety standards.

## 7. Health, safety and wellbeing

- The Health and Safety at Work Act applies to all employees, regardless of location.
- Employees must ensure they have a safe and suitable home working environment, free from avoidable disruption.
- A risk assessment (self-assessment or Loaded Dice-led) must be completed before home working begins and reviewed periodically.
- Employees must follow safe working practices and report concerns to their manager.
- Failure to comply with health and safety requirements may result in withdrawal of home working arrangements.

## 8. Insurance and financial responsibilities

- Loaded Dice-issued equipment is covered by the Loaded Dice insurance. Employees must take reasonable precautions to protect it.
- Employees remain responsible for their own home and contents insurance and for notifying insurers, landlords or mortgage providers if required.
- Loaded Dice will not be liable for costs arising from employees' failure to secure such permissions.
- Expenses related to home working (e.g. broadband contribution) will be agreed in the Home Working Agreement and reimbursed in line with Loaded Dice policy.

## 9. Confidentiality, security and data protection

- Employees must comply with the Loaded Dice Data Protection and IT Security policies at all times.
- Loaded Dice information must be stored securely and kept confidential.
- Employees must use Loaded Dice-approved systems (e.g. VPN, cloud storage, MFA) and must not transfer information via personal email or devices without authorisation.
- Breaches of confidentiality or data security will be treated as a disciplinary matter.

## 10. Hours of work, leave and absence

- Employees and their line manager will agree working patterns in the Home Working Agreement.
- Employees must take appropriate rest breaks and comply with Working Time Regulations.
- Requests for annual leave and reporting of sickness absence must follow the normal Loaded Dice procedures.

## 11. Monitoring and performance

- Home workers will be managed based on outcomes and performance standards, not physical presence.
- Regular communication (e.g. team meetings, one-to-ones) will be maintained to support collaboration and wellbeing.
- Loaded Dice reserves the right to review, vary or withdraw home working arrangements where they no longer meet business or performance needs.

## 12. Policy review

This policy will be reviewed every two years, or earlier if legislation or business needs change.
